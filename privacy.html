Privacy Policy

Polymarket Rclone Backup is a personal-use application.

The application accesses Google Drive only after authorization by its owner and is used for encrypted backup and archival storage.

Data is not sold or shared with third parties.

Credentials and authorization tokens are not intentionally exposed publicly.

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time through Google Account permissions.

For questions, contact the repository owner through the GitHub repository.
